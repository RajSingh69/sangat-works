Why We Built This

Keeping opportunity within the community.

Many Sikhs need trusted services, skilled tradespeople, professionals and business support, but don't always know where to find them within the community. Whether someone needs a builder, accountant, designer, coach, consultant, developer or local business, Sangat Works helps the community find and support one another.

1

Support Sikh Businesses

Help keep money, skills and opportunities circulating within the Sangat.

2

Build Trust

Profiles, reviews, verification badges and community recommendations help people make better decisions.

3

Create Opportunities

Helping businesses grow, professionals connect and younger members of the community discover pathways.

Our Mission

Technology for real-world community support.

Sangat Works isn't just a directory. It's a growing community platform for business support, professional networking, trusted recommendations, Gurdwara-linked opportunities and community-led features.

Built So Far

Business profiles, directory and map search, reviews, verification badges, featured listings, projects, skills pools, messaging, Young Professionals and member rankings.

Coming Next

More Gurdwara networking, jobs and opportunities, mentorship, community projects and tools shaped by member feedback.

Built Independently

Built with a community-first mindset: practical tools, real value and long-term support for the Sangat.
