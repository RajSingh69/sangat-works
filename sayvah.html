What is SayVah?

Built for seva, support and trust.

SayVah is a dedicated community app for Sikh seva, Gurdwara connection and local support. Members of the Sangat can ask for help, offer their time, discover community activity and build trusted connections.

1

Request Help

Create a seva request when you need support from the community.

2

Offer Seva

Browse available seva opportunities and offer help where you can.

3

Gurdwara Connection

Stay connected with your local Gurdwaras, their events and community activity.

4

Trust Profiles

Profiles, trust scores and community activity help keep the app safe and reliable.

5

Community Pulse

See what's happening in the community and where support is needed most.

6

Admin Tools

Gurdwara and community admins can manage requests, review activity and support users.
