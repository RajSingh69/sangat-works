Charities

Supporting the causes that support the Sangat.

Seva and giving back are at the heart of Sikhi. Here are charities and causes our community cares about, with direct links so you can donate or get involved.

Humanitarian Relief

Khalsa Aid

Khalsa Aid is an international humanitarian relief charity founded on the Sikh principle of recognising the whole human race as one. It provides aid to people affected by natural disasters, conflict and hardship around the world.

Donate to Khalsa Aid khalsaaid.org/donate Khalsa Aid website www.khalsaaid.org

Remembering Our Shaheeds

Shaheed Parivars

Many families of Sikh Shaheeds, those who gave their lives for Sikhi, justice and human rights, continue to live with the loss and its hardship. Supporting these families, and simply standing with them, is a way for the Sangat to honour their sacrifice. Watch their stories, donate, or send a message of thanks.

Donate via Khalsa Aid khalsaaid.org/donate Watch, Donate & Send Thanks
